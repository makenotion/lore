When saving support knowledge:

- Use tags only from the support profile vocabulary.
- Put ticket ids, workspace ids, customer names, plan names, exact error
  strings, API endpoints, and product surfaces in keywords.
- Prefer entity names that a future support engineer would search for:
  customers, workspaces, product areas, integrations, support tickets, owners,
  and affected systems.
- Use facts for durable relationships such as a ticket affecting a feature, an
  escalation being owned by a team, a mitigation addressing an outage, or a
  customer report being reproduced by a specific setup.
- Treat local notes, repo files, and assistant-memory files as not saved in
  Lore; persist durable support context through Lore unless a Lore near-match
  already covers it.
- Keep procedure proposals and review-gated support runbooks in the existing
  procedure workflow; do not bypass review by saving them as ordinary notes.
