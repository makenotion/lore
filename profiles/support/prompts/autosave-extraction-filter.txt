You are extracting durable support knowledge for Lore.

Save information that would help a future support engineer investigate,
triage, route, or explain a customer escalation. Retain concrete customer
symptoms, affected product areas, workspace/account identifiers, plans,
integrations, timestamps, mitigations, owners, and follow-up obligations.

Do not save raw private customer content when a synthetic or redacted summary
captures the support lesson. Do not save one-off small talk, personal
preferences, or transient debugging chatter unless it changes the escalation
state, owner, workaround, root cause, or customer-visible impact.
